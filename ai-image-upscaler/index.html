Questions

How do I upscale an image with AI?

Pick 2x or 4x above the drop area, then drop the photo or paste it with Ctrl+V. The AI model runs in your browser, shows the result next to the original and saves a PNG twice or four times as wide and tall.

How do I make an image higher resolution?

Upscale it: 2x turns a 1000 × 750 photo into 2000 × 1500 pixels, 4x into 4000 × 3000. Plain resizing just stretches pixels; the AI upscaler adds sharp edges and detail, so the bigger photo stays clean.

2x or 4x: which should I choose?

2x for most photos: it is faster and looks natural. 4x for very small images such as thumbnails, avatars or old web pictures under 600 pixels.

Is there a size limit?

Photos up to 4 megapixels (about 2300 × 1700). That is exactly where small images need upscaling; a photo bigger than that is already high resolution.

Is it free and private?

Yes. Free, no sign-up, no watermark. The photo is upscaled on your own device and never uploaded; only the AI model is downloaded once.
