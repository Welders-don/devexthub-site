## Title
How to Copy Text From a Picture: 2 Free Ways in Chrome

## Описание
Need to copy text from a picture but the words won't select? Here are two free ways to do it in
Chrome, without retyping anything.

If the picture is on a web page, open Extract Text from Image, hit Start selection and draw a box
around it. If the picture is a file on your computer, choose Upload File and pick it. Either way the
OCR reads every line right on your device, so nothing is uploaded to a server. Then copy the text or
save it as a clean .txt file.

Works with photos, screenshots, JPG and PNG images, receipts, invoices and slides.
