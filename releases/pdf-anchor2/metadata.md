## Описание
Bank statements arrive as PDFs, so the rows are locked inside the page. Copy them into Excel and the
columns fall apart, which is why most people end up retyping every line by hand.

This is the fast way. Click the extension icon, the side panel opens, drop the statement in, click
convert. Two pages turn into one clean sheet with every transaction in place: dates, descriptions,
debits, credits and the running balance, each in its own column. Download it as .xlsx or .csv and
start working.

Runs in Chrome, no login and no upload to a random website.
