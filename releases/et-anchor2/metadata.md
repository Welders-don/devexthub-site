## Title
How to Copy Text From an Image in Chrome (Free, No Retyping)

## Описание
You can see the words in an image but you can't select them. This is how to copy text from an image
in one click, without retyping a single line.

Extract Text from Image is a free Chrome extension. Click the icon, hit Start selection, draw a box
over the picture, and the text comes out as real, selectable text. The OCR runs on your device, so
the image is never uploaded to a server. Copy it, or download a clean .txt file.

Works on screenshots, photos, slides, infographics and scanned pages, in dozens of languages.

Get it free: https://www.devexthub.com/extract-text-from-image/
