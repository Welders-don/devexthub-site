## Title
JPG to Text, Free: Copy the Words From Any Image (Chrome)

## Описание
Got a JPG but the words are stuck inside it? This is how to turn a JPG into real, selectable text
for free, without retyping a thing.

Extract Text from Image is a free Chrome extension. Open it, choose Upload File, pick your JPG, and
the text comes straight out. The OCR runs on your device, so the image is never uploaded to a server.
Copy the text, or download a clean .txt file.

Works with JPG, PNG and screenshots, photos, receipts, invoices and scanned pages, in dozens of
languages.
