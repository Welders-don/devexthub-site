Questions

How do I convert MOV to MP3?

Drop the MOV video into the converter at the top of this page, keep MP3 selected, pick the quality and press Convert. The MP3 lands in your Downloads and plays right on the page. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I convert MP4 or other videos to MP3 too?

Yes. MP4, MOV, MKV and WEBM all work, as long as your browser can read the sound inside. iPhone videos (MOV with AAC sound) work in every modern browser.

MP3, WAV or M4A: which one should I pick?

MP3 plays everywhere and stays small: 192 kbps is plenty for speech and music. WAV is uncompressed, for editing in an audio app, and is about ten times bigger. M4A (AAC) is a little smaller than MP3 at the same quality and plays on Apple devices.

Is there a length or size limit?

No fixed limit. Long videos are fine for MP3 and M4A; WAV of a very long video can get too big for a browser tab, and the converter tells you before you start.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The sound is taken out and converted on your own device, inside the browser tab.
