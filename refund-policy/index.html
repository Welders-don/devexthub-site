Refund Policy

Product: Transcribe Video to Text paid plans (Lite, Pro)
Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. 14-day refund

If the paid plan does not work for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full, as long as you have used less than 30 minutes of AI transcription on the paid plan. Every minute of AI transcription costs us real money with our speech recognition provider, which is why the refund is tied to usage.

2. Renewals

The same rule applies to an automatic renewal: if you forgot to cancel, write to us within 14 days of the renewal charge, and if you have used less than 30 minutes since the renewal, we will refund it.

3. When something is broken

If the extension does not work for you because of a problem on our side and we cannot fix it, we will refund your last payment regardless of usage.

4. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future charges. You keep your plan until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling by itself does not refund the current period; for that, see the rules above.

5. How to ask for a refund

Write to team@devexthub.com from the email you paid with, or reply to your Paddle receipt. Payments are processed by our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com, so the refund is issued by Paddle to your original payment method. It usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country.
