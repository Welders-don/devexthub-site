## Description (основное поле)
Transcribe Video to Text is a free Chrome extension that turns any video into a clean, readable transcript in a single click. It works on YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom and most sites with a video player. You get a speaker-by-speaker layout with timestamps, and can export to Word, TXT or SRT for subtitles. Supports 50+ languages, runs right in the browser, and needs no account or sign-up. Free to use.

Great for students pulling quotes from lectures, researchers, journalists, and content creators repurposing video into posts.
