## Long description (для площадок с большим полем)
Transcribe Video to Text is a free Chrome extension that turns any video into a clean, readable transcript in a single click. Instead of copy-pasting or paying for a heavy transcription service, you open a video and get the full text laid out speaker by speaker, with timestamps you can jump from.

It works where you already are: YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom and most sites with a video player. When you need the transcript elsewhere, export it to Word, TXT or SRT for subtitles.

Key points:
- One-click transcription of any video in the browser
- Clean speaker-by-speaker layout with timestamps
- Export to Word, TXT or SRT
- 50+ languages
- No sign-up, no account
- Free

Great for students pulling quotes from lectures, researchers, journalists, content creators repurposing video into posts, and anyone who prefers reading over watching.
