## 1. TVT (Transcribe Video to Text)
- Картинки: `releases/fazier-tvt/` logo-250 + gallery-1-hero, gallery-2-demo, gallery-3-how
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv99xOSFys8
- Topics: Productivity · Chrome Extensions · Artificial Intelligence (или Education, если AI нет)

Features:
Transcript of YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom, Coursera and most web videos
Text laid out by speaker with timestamps
Export to Word, TXT or SRT subtitles
50+ languages
YouTube transcripts free and unlimited
Works in the side panel, no need to leave the tab

Use cases:
Students turning lectures and courses into notes
Creators pulling quotes or subtitles from their own videos
Researchers and journalists transcribing interviews and podcasts
Anyone who wants to read a long video instead of watching it

Competitors: Tactiq, Glasp, Notta, Otter.ai

Makers comment:
Hi Fazier! I built this because copying a transcript out of YouTube by hand is painful, and most tools stop at one plain block of text. This one gives you a readable script split by speaker with timestamps, and exports straight to Word or SRT. YouTube captions are free and unlimited. For videos without captions it runs AI speech recognition, with a free monthly allowance. Would love to hear which sites you want it to support next.

## 2. ET (Extract Text from Image)
- Картинки: `releases/fazier-et/` logo-250 + gallery-1-hero, gallery-2-demo, gallery-3-how
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YBuAdO9E4cU
- Topics: Productivity · Chrome Extensions · Privacy (или Developer Tools, если Privacy нет)

Features:
Copy text from any image, screenshot or photo
OCR runs on your device, images are never uploaded
Upload a file or paste a picture with Ctrl+V
Dozens of languages: Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Thai and more
Copy all or download as .txt
Free, no account

Use cases:
Grabbing text from a screenshot of a chat, error message or slide
Copying numbers from a photo of a receipt or invoice
Pulling quotes out of infographics and scanned pages
Working with private documents you do not want to upload anywhere

Competitors: Copyfish, Google Lens, Text Blaze OCR, Project Naptha

Makers comment:
Hi Fazier! You know that moment when the text is right there on the screen but you cannot select it? That is what this fixes. Paste or upload an image and get copyable text in a second. The part I cared about most: recognition runs in your browser, so screenshots of private chats or documents never go to a server. It is free with no account. Tell me which language it reads worst for you, that is what I am tuning next.

## 3. IE (Image Enhancer)
- Картинки: `releases/fazier-ie/` logo-250 + 4 gallery-*
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QtE2CfgRJHo
- Topics: Design Tools · Chrome Extensions · Artificial Intelligence (или Photography)

Features:
Upscale small images up to 4x
Unblur and clean up compression artifacts
AI model runs on your device, nothing is uploaded
No watermark, no daily limit
Free, no sign-up

Use cases:
Enlarging product photos to meet marketplace size requirements
Fixing thumbnails, avatars and images saved from chats
Cleaning up blurry screenshots before sharing
Upscaling private photos without sending them to a cloud service

Competitors: Let's Enhance, Upscale.media, Upscayl, Pixelcut

Makers comment:
Hi Fazier! Most online upscalers want you to upload your photo, then add a watermark or a daily cap. This one runs the AI model right in your browser: no upload, no watermark, no limit. To be honest about where it works: it is great on small or compressed images, but it cannot rescue a shot that was out of focus in the camera. Feedback on tough images is very welcome.
