PDF
- Tagline: Turn PDF tables into Excel or CSV in one click
- Description: A free Chrome extension for anyone who retypes tables from PDFs. Open a bank statement, invoice or report, click once and get a clean Excel or CSV file. It also converts Excel back to PDF. An optional AI mode handles messy scanned tables. No account needed.

TVT
- Tagline: Get the text of any video without leaving the tab
- Description: A free Chrome extension that turns YouTube, Vimeo, Loom and most web videos into readable text with timestamps. Export to Word, TXT or SRT. YouTube captions are free and unlimited; AI transcription for other videos works in 50+ languages with a free monthly allowance.

ET
- Tagline: Copy text out of any screenshot or photo
- Description: A free Chrome extension that reads the text in an image, screenshot or photo so you can copy it or save it as TXT. Recognition runs on your own device, so images never leave your computer. Many languages, no account.

IE
- Tagline: Fix blurry photos right in your browser
- Description: A free Chrome extension that unblurs, sharpens and upscales low-quality photos. The AI model runs on your device, so nothing is uploaded, and there is no watermark and no daily limit.

PDF
- Tagline: PDF tables to Excel/CSV, parsed in the browser
- Description: Chrome extension that detects tables in PDF files and exports them to XLSX or CSV, plus XLSX to PDF. Parsing happens in the browser; an optional AI mode helps with messy or scanned tables. Free, no account.

TVT
- Tagline: Video to timestamped text, from any tab
- Description: Chrome extension that reads YouTube captions directly and runs AI speech-to-text for other web players (Vimeo, Loom and more). Output with timestamps, export to Word, TXT, SRT. Free tier: YouTube unlimited, AI transcription with a monthly allowance.

ET
- Tagline: On-device OCR for images and screenshots
- Description: Chrome extension that runs OCR locally, so images are never sent to a server. Select an image or screenshot, get copyable text or a TXT file. Many languages, free, no account.

IE
- Tagline: On-device AI upscaling for photos in Chrome
- Description: Chrome extension that runs an image-enhancement model locally to unblur, sharpen and upscale photos. No uploads, no watermark, no limits. Free.
