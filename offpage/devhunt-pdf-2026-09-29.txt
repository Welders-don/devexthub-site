[Tool name]
Convert PDF to Excel

[Slogan / Tagline]
PDF tables to Excel or CSV, parsed in the browser

[Website URL]
https://www.devexthub.com/pdf-to-excel/?utm_source=devhunt&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=offpage_pdftoexcel

[Description]
Chrome extension that detects tables in PDF files and exports them to XLSX or CSV, plus XLSX back to PDF. Parsing happens in the browser. An optional AI mode helps with scanned or messy tables like bank statements and invoices. Free, no account.
