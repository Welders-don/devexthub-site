Image Enhancer is a free Chrome extension that fixes blurry, low-quality photos in one click. It unblurs, sharpens and upscales image resolution using on-device AI, so your photos are never uploaded to a server. No watermark, no limits, no account, no sign-up. Honest enhancement — sharper, clearer images in seconds, right in your browser.
