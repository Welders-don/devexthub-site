What happens to your data

Straight answer, because it depends on the platform.

  • On YouTube and Vimeo, the transcript is pulled from the video's own captions inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • On other sites, the audio stream is transcribed by an AI service as it plays. It is processed in the moment and not stored.
  • Your browsing history is never recorded, and your data is never sold or shared.

Questions

How do I transcribe a video to text in Chrome?

Install the extension, open any video, and click the icon. The transcript appears in the side panel in seconds. No account, no file upload.

How do I get a transcript of a YouTube video?

Open the video, click the extension, and the full transcript is built for you. It works whether or not the video has auto-generated captions, and you can copy or export it as text.

Does YouTube automatically transcribe videos?

YouTube generates captions for many videos, but they are hard to copy and often incomplete. This extension pulls a clean, complete transcript in one click and lets you search, copy or export it.

Can ChatGPT transcribe a YouTube video?

ChatGPT can't watch a video or capture its audio on its own. This extension does the transcription right in your browser, then you can paste that text into ChatGPT to summarize or analyze it.

Can I export the transcript to Word?

Yes. Save it as a formatted .docx Word document, or as TXT, CSV or SRT subtitles. Most transcription tools only hand you plain text, so this is one of the main reasons people switch.

Does it separate speakers?

Yes. The transcript is laid out speaker by speaker with timestamps, so it reads like a clean script instead of one long block of text.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom, Coursera and most streaming sites, including content behind a login. It's a transcript-of-any-video tool, not limited to one platform.

Which languages does it support?

Over 50 languages. The AI handles accents, technical terms and fast speech with high accuracy.

Is it free?

Yes, it's free to use. YouTube transcripts are unlimited. Just install it and start transcribing, no sign-up required.
