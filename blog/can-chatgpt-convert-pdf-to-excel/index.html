Can ChatGPT convert a PDF to Excel?

Convert PDF to Excel · 3 min read

Short answer: it can try, but you shouldn't trust it with a table of numbers. Here's why, and the reliable way to get a PDF's data into Excel exactly as it appears.

Where ChatGPT falls short

ChatGPT is a language model. With a paragraph it's great; with a grid of figures it tends to paraphrase, it can reorder rows, merge or drop columns, round values, or quietly invent a number to fill a gap. For an invoice or a bank statement, one wrong figure is a real problem, and you often can't tell which one changed.

There's a privacy angle too: to use ChatGPT you'd be uploading the document to a third party, which isn't ideal for financial or personal files.

The reliable way: a one-click extractor

Convert PDF to Excel is a free Chrome extension built for exactly this. It reads the table's structure and rebuilds it, instead of paraphrasing it.
