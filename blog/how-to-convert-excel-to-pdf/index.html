How to convert Excel to PDF free, without losing formatting

Convert PDF to Excel · 4 min read

You need to send a spreadsheet as a clean PDF, not an editable file, and you don't want the columns spilling onto three extra pages. Here's how to get a tidy, one-page-fit PDF in a click.

The usual pain with Excel to PDF is layout: a wide sheet breaks across pages, half a column lands on page two, and the result looks messy. The fix is setting the page fit before you export, or letting a tool handle it for you.

The fastest way: a one-click Chrome extension

Convert PDF to Excel works both ways. As well as pulling tables out of a PDF, it turns an Excel sheet into a clean PDF, right in your browser.

  1. Add the extension to Chrome and open your Excel file.
  2. Click the extension and choose Excel to PDF.
  3. It applies a clean page fit so columns don't get cut off.
  4. Download a shareable PDF, no account, no sign-up.

Or do it inside Excel

If you'd rather use Excel directly, you can, it just takes a couple more steps to avoid the layout breaking:

  1. Select your data, then set Page Layout → Print Area.
  2. Under Page Layout → Scale to Fit, set Width to 1 page so columns don't wrap.
  3. Go to File → Save As (or Export) and choose PDF.

That keeps the formatting intact. The extension just does the page-fit part automatically so you don't have to think about it.

Keeping the formatting

Most "lost formatting" problems come down to one thing: the sheet is wider than the page. Fit the columns to one page wide before exporting and the PDF comes out clean, whichever method you use.
