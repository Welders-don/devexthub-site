How to make a blurry picture clear

Image Enhancer · 2 min read

A slightly soft or low-resolution photo can be sharpened up a good deal, and you do not need Photoshop or a paid site to do it. Here is how to make a picture clearer in one click, plus an honest note on what is and is not fixable.

Pictures come out blurry for a few reasons, and they split into two groups. Either the camera missed focus, your hand moved or the light was low, in which case the detail never reached the sensor. Or the image was saved small, compressed hard and then blown up, in which case the detail was captured and then thrown away by the file format. An AI enhancer can rebuild the second kind, because there is still structure in the pixels to work from. The first kind it cannot touch, and no amount of processing will change that.

What it can fix, and what it can't

We tested this rather than guessing at it. These improve well, far more than simply resizing them would:

  • Small or low-resolution images that turn to mush when enlarged.
  • Pictures squeezed by heavy JPEG compression, the sort that arrive from a chat app.
  • Screenshots and thumbnails saved at a fraction of the size you now need.

These do not come back, and the measured gain was near zero: a badly out-of-focus shot, heavy motion blur, and scanned film. On film the enhancer actually smooths the grain away instead of helping, because it reads that texture as noise. The tool works from what is in the pixels, so if the information was never recorded it cannot be restored. This is honest enhancement, not the movie-style "zoom and enhance".

Why do it in the browser

Online photo fixers usually upload your picture, add a watermark or cap you at a few free images. Doing it in an extension keeps the photo on your device, with no watermark and no daily limit. It is quicker too: the image is already in your tab.
