How to insert a PDF into Excel

By Nichola Chaus · Published July 25, 2026 · 4 min read

"Insert a PDF into Excel" can mean two different things, and the right method depends on which you want: attach the PDF file to the sheet, or get the PDF's numbers into cells you can edit. Here's both.

Option 1: Embed the PDF as an object

If you just want the PDF to travel with the workbook, say, attaching a receipt next to a row, embed it as an object:

  1. Click the cell where you want it, then go to Insert → Object.
  2. Choose Create from File and browse to your PDF.
  3. Tick Display as icon for a small file icon, or leave it unticked for a first-page preview.
  4. Click OK. The PDF is now attached to the sheet.

The catch: it's just a preview or an icon. You can't edit, sort or sum anything inside it, it's a file glued on top of the grid, not data.

Option 2: Extract the table into real cells

If what you actually want is the PDF's numbers in the spreadsheet, in their own cells and editable, you need to extract the table, not embed the file.

Convert PDF to Excel is a free Chrome extension that does this in one click:

  1. Click the extension icon, then drag your PDF into the panel that opens and click Convert to Excel.
  2. It rebuilds the table into an editable .xlsx or CSV, column by column.
  3. Paste the result into your workbook.
