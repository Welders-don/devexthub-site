How to unblur a picture

Image Enhancer · 3 min read

A photo came out soft, and you wish it were sharp. Here's how to unblur a picture in one click, without Photoshop or an upload site that stamps a watermark on the result.

Blur happens when detail gets lost, slightly out of focus, a small low-resolution image, a soft phone shot. AI enhancement rebuilds that fine detail, so edges tighten up and the picture reads clearly again.

The fastest way: a one-click Chrome extension

Image Enhancer is a free AI Chrome extension that sharpens and upscales a blurry picture, right in your browser.

  1. Add the extension to Chrome and open it.
  2. Drop in your blurry picture, a JPG or PNG.
  3. The on-device AI sharpens the detail and upscales the image in a click.
  4. Compare before and after, then download the clearer version, no watermark.

Your photo is enhanced on your device, so it's never uploaded to a server.

How much blur can it fix?

Soft focus, mild motion and low-resolution softness improve the most, that covers most everyday photos. A severely smeared or completely out-of-focus shot has less detail to recover, so expect a good improvement rather than a miracle. It's honest enhancement, not repainting the image.
