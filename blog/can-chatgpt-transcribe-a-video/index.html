Can ChatGPT transcribe a video?

Transcribe Video to Text · 3 min read

Short answer: not by itself. ChatGPT can't watch a video or listen to its audio. But there's a simple two-step workflow that gets you exactly what you want, the video summarized, quoted or translated by AI.

Why ChatGPT can't do it alone

ChatGPT works on text. It has no way to open a YouTube link, play a video, or turn speech into words. If you paste a video URL and ask for a transcript, you'll get a guess or a refusal, not the real thing. The transcription has to happen somewhere else first.

The workflow that actually works

Get the transcript in your browser, then hand the text to ChatGPT:

  1. Open the video and use Transcribe Video to Text, a free Chrome extension, to build the full transcript in one click.
  2. Copy the transcript, or export it to Word, TXT or SRT.
  3. Paste the text into ChatGPT and ask it to summarize, translate, or pull out the key quotes.

The transcription runs in your browser; ChatGPT just works on the clean text you give it. It's free, needs no sign-up, and works on YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom and most sites.

Why not just screenshot or retype it?

Because it's slow and error-prone. A transcript tool gives you the complete text, laid out by speaker with timestamps, so you can drop it straight into ChatGPT without cleaning it up first. That means better summaries, accurate quotes and no missed lines.
