How to transcribe a TikTok video to text

Transcribe Video to Text · 3 min read

TikToks move fast, and the words matter, a recipe, a tip, a quote you want to save. Here's how to turn any TikTok into clean, copyable text in one click, without retyping it or screen-recording.

TikTok shows auto-captions on some videos, but they flash by, run together, and there's no way to copy them out. A transcript tool reads the video that's playing and hands you the full text you can actually use.

The fastest way: a one-click Chrome extension

Transcribe Video to Text is a free Chrome extension that transcribes the TikTok you're watching, right on the page. No download, no account, no link-pasting.

  1. Add the extension to Chrome and open any TikTok in your browser.
  2. Click the extension icon. It reads the video and builds the transcript in seconds.
  3. The text appears in a side panel, laid out speaker by speaker with timestamps.
  4. Copy what you need, or export the whole thing to Word, TXT or SRT.

What you can do with the transcript

  • Save a quote or a recipe without pausing and retyping every line.
  • Repurpose it into a caption, a blog post or your own script.
  • Export to Word (.docx) or plain TXT, or SRT if you want subtitles.
  • Summarize it with AI, paste the transcript into ChatGPT and ask for the key points.

Does TikTok have a transcript feature?

TikTok can auto-generate captions for creators, but as a viewer there's no clean "copy the transcript" button, and plenty of videos have captions switched off. A dedicated tool gives you the complete text in one click, in a format you can copy, search or export.

Works on more than TikTok

The same extension transcribes video across most of the web, YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Coursera and most sites where a video plays in your browser. Read our guide on getting a YouTube transcript for the same steps on YouTube.
