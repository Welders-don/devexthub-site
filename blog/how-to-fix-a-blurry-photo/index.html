How to fix a blurry photo

Image Enhancer · 4 min read

A photo you wanted came out blurry. Before you write it off, most everyday blur can be cleaned up in one click. Here's why photos come out blurry, and how to fix it.

Why is your photo blurry?

  • Missed focus, the camera locked onto the wrong spot.
  • Motion, the camera or the subject moved during the shot.
  • Low light, a slow shutter softened the whole frame.
  • Low resolution, a small image looks soft and pixelated when enlarged.
  • Compression, the file was squeezed on its way through a chat, an upload or a screenshot.

This distinction decides whether the photo can be saved at all. The last two are digital damage: the detail was captured, then degraded, and AI enhancement can rebuild a good deal of it. The first three are optical: the detail never reached the sensor. Nothing can recover information that was never recorded, so a missed-focus or motion-smeared shot will not come back sharp no matter which tool you try.

Set your expectations

We put numbers on this instead of hand-waving. On an image that was shrunk and compressed hard, the enhancer recovers roughly four times the sharpness. On an optically out-of-focus shot the gain is close to nothing, and on scanned film it smooths the grain away rather than helping.

So a small or over-compressed photo can come back looking genuinely sharp. A smeared or badly focused one will not, and it is fairer to tell you that now than to have you wait for a result that cannot exist. The tool rebuilds what is still in the file, it does not invent a new scene.
