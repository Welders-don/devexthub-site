How to get a YouTube transcript, free and in one click

Transcribe Video to Text · 4 min read

You want the words out of a YouTube video, to quote it, study it, or feed it to an AI. Here's the fastest way to get a clean, full transcript with timestamps, for free, without pasting the link into a sketchy website.

YouTube does caption a lot of videos, but those captions are built for reading along, not for copying. There's no easy "select all", the punctuation is patchy, and plenty of videos have no captions at all. A transcript tool solves that: it reads the video for you and hands back the complete text in one click.

The fastest way: a one-click Chrome extension

Transcribe Video to Text is a free Chrome extension that builds the full transcript of the video you're watching, right on the page. No file to upload, no account, no link-pasting.

  1. Add the extension to Chrome and open any YouTube video.
  2. Click the extension icon. It reads the video and builds the transcript in seconds.
  3. The text appears in a side panel, laid out speaker by speaker with timestamps.
  4. Copy the part you need, or export the whole thing to Word, TXT or SRT.

Because it works in your browser, YouTube transcripts are unlimited and free, and it handles videos with or without auto-captions in over 50 languages.

What you can do with the transcript

  • Copy a clean quote without retyping it or fixing the punctuation by hand.
  • Search the whole talk to jump straight to the moment something was said.
  • Export to Word (.docx) or plain TXT for notes, or SRT if you need subtitles for the video.
  • Summarize it with AI, paste the transcript into ChatGPT or another tool and ask for the key points.

Does YouTube automatically transcribe videos?

For many videos, yes, YouTube auto-generates captions. But they're meant to be watched, not exported: there's no bulk copy, the text runs together without punctuation, and some videos have captions turned off. A dedicated transcript tool gives you the full text in a format you can actually use, in one click.

Can ChatGPT transcribe a YouTube video?

Not on its own. ChatGPT can't open a video or listen to its audio. The workflow that does work: generate the transcript first with a browser tool like the one above, then paste that text into ChatGPT to summarize, translate or pull out quotes. The transcription happens in your browser; ChatGPT just works on the text.

What about TikTok, Vimeo and other videos?

The same extension works across most of the web, not just YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Loom, Coursera and most sites where a video plays in your browser. It's a transcript-of-any-video tool, so the steps above are the same wherever you're watching.
