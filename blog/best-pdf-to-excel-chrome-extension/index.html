The best PDF to Excel Chrome extension

PDF to Excel · 4 min read

Every PDF to Excel tool claims to convert your table. The real question is what comes out the other side. Rather than a ranked list that ages badly, here is what separates a usable converter from one that costs you an afternoon of cleanup, plus a free pick that ticks the boxes.

What to look for in a PDF to Excel extension

The failure modes are always the same, so judge on these:

  • Columns survive. The classic bad result is every row landing in column A. A good converter rebuilds the grid from the page layout.
  • Numbers stay numbers. If totals arrive as text, you cannot sum them and the sheet is useless for anything financial.
  • Scanned pages handled. Half the invoices and bank statements in the world are scans with no text layer. That needs a different path from a text PDF, and plenty of tools quietly fail on it.
  • No file round trip. Uploading a bank statement to an unknown website to get a spreadsheet back is a poor trade.
  • Both directions. Sooner or later you also need the sheet back as a PDF.

Other options

Several store listings that look like extensions are simply a shortcut to one of those websites. An extension that does the conversion itself skips the round trip entirely.
