Questions

How do I make a blurry picture clear?

If the picture looks blurry because it is small or heavily compressed, drop it into the enhancer and the on-device AI rebuilds the fine detail as it enlarges it. Compare before and after, then save it. If the shot itself was taken out of focus, see the question below.

Will it fix an out-of-focus or scanned film photo?

No, and we would rather say so up front. We measured it: on a digitally degraded image the enhancer recovers around 4x the sharpness, but on an optically out-of-focus shot the gain is close to nothing, and on scanned film it smooths away the grain instead of helping. Those files have no fine detail left to recover, and any tool claiming otherwise is inventing pixels. That is a different problem and needs a different kind of model.

How do I make a picture less blurry?

Drop it in and the enhancer rebuilds the soft, blocky areas and upscales the resolution in one click, without you touching any sliders. It works best when the softness comes from the image being small or over-compressed.

Can I make a product photo big enough for a listing?

Yes, that is the main thing people use it for. Enlarge the shot up to 4x so it clears the marketplace size requirement, with no watermark and no sign-up on the way.

Can I upscale an image without losing quality?

Yes. Instead of just stretching the pixels, the AI adds detail as it enlarges, so a small image gets bigger and cleaner rather than blocky.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
