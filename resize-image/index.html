Questions

How do I change the size of an image?

Drop the image into the resizer at the top of this page, type the new width or height (the other side follows to keep proportions) or pick a percentage, then download. You see the new size and file weight before you save.

How do I make an image file smaller in KB?

Pick JPG or WEBP and lower the quality slider, or make the dimensions smaller. The new file size is shown live, so you can stop when it fits an upload limit.

Will resizing make my image blurry?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Making it much bigger only stretches the pixels; for a bigger and sharper picture use our AI image upscaler instead.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WEBP in and out. PNG keeps transparency; JPG fills transparent areas with white.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is resized in your browser, on your own device.
