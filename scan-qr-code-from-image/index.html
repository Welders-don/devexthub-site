Questions

How do I scan a QR code from a picture?

Drop the picture or screenshot into the scanner at the top of this page, or paste it with Ctrl+V. The content of the QR code appears right away: a link you can check and open, or text you can copy.

How do I scan a QR code from a screenshot on my phone?

Open this page on your phone, tap Choose image and pick the screenshot from your gallery. It works in the phone's browser, no app needed.

Is it safe to open the link from a QR code?

Check the address before you open it: the scanner shows the full link and its domain first. Scam QR codes often lead to look-alike addresses.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The QR code is read in your browser, on your own device.

Is it free?

Yes. Free, no sign-up, no app to install.
