Questions

How do I unblur an image?

Drop the photo into the tool at the top of this page or paste it with Ctrl+V. The AI sharpens edges and removes the soft, smeared look, then shows the result next to the original so you can compare before downloading.

Can it fix a very blurry photo?

It works best on slight blur: a soft focus, a small shake, a picture that lost sharpness after compression. A photo that is heavily out of focus or motion-smeared has lost the detail, and no tool can bring it back honestly.

Can it make blurry text readable?

Often, yes, when the letters are only a little soft or pixelated, such as a screenshot or a photo of a document. Then use our image to text converter to copy the words.

How do I unpixelate an image?

Drop it here: the AI smooths the blocky pixels into clean edges. For a very small pixelated picture, the AI image upscaler with 4x works even better.

Is it free and private?

Yes. Free, no sign-up, no watermark. The photo is processed on your own device and never uploaded.
