Questions

How do I convert an image to Word?

Drop the picture, screenshot or scan into the converter at the top of this page, or paste it with Ctrl+V. The text is recognized in a few seconds; check it, then press Download Word to get an editable .docx file.

Can I convert a PNG or JPG to Word?

Yes. PNG, JPG and WEBP all work. Each line of text in the picture becomes a paragraph in the Word document, so you can edit and format it right away.

Is the layout of the picture kept?

The text is kept line by line; fonts, columns and images from the picture are not recreated. For tables with rows and columns, use our PDF to Excel converter on a PDF instead.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and more: pick the language above the drop area.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The text is recognized and the Word file is built in your browser, on your own device.
