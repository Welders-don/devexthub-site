Questions

How do I change the color of something in a picture?

Drop the image into the color changer above, click the color you want to change, then choose the new color. Everything of that color changes at once, keeping its shadows and highlights. Widen or narrow How similar to catch more or fewer shades.

Can I recolor an image without Photoshop?

Yes. This works in any browser with no install and no account: click, pick a color, download.

Why didn't the background change?

Grey, white and black areas have no color to shift, so they are left as they are on purpose. Tick Change all colors to shift every colored pixel in the picture instead.

Can I make something black or white?

Partly: picking a very dark or light new color darkens or lightens the area, but shadows are kept, so the result stays natural rather than flat.

Is my image uploaded?

No. It is recolored in your browser, on your own device, and saved in the same format.
